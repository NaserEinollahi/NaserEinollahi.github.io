01 — ABOUT

From materials to systems.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Mechanical Engineering at the National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST). My research focuses on solid-state hydrogen storage materials, particularly magnesium-based hydrides and high-entropy alloy catalysts.

My work spans hydrogen sorption characterization, thermal management of hydrogen-storage systems, numerical simulation, and first-principles calculations, with an emphasis on scalable hydrogen-energy technologies.
